# Samesies Project Brief

## Vision

Samesies is a local hobby-discovery platform that helps people find nearby people who share their hobbies, interests, passions, and wonderfully specific obsessions.

Trust is earned through participation, not extracted from legal identity.

---

## One-Sentence Concept

Samesies is a local hobby-discovery platform where people find others nearby who are into the same things, connect privately, and build trust through genuine community participation.

---

## The Problem

It is easy to find thousands of people online who share an interest.

It is surprisingly difficult to find the handful of people nearby who share that same interest and might actually want to do it together.

---

## The Goal

Create the moment:

> "Wait, you're into that too? Samesies!"

The platform exists to help people discover genuine local connections through shared interests and move those connections into the real world.

---

## Core Principles

### Local First

The value comes from discovering nearby people, not building another global audience.

### Privacy First

Collect the minimum useful information.

Never expose precise residential location or personal contact information.

### Behavior Over Identity

Trust should come from participation and consistency, not government identification.

### Real Life Over Screen Time

Success means users leave the platform and enjoy their hobby.

### Evidence Over Ratings

Use participation records and factual interaction signals instead of popularity systems.

---

## What Samesies Is Not

Samesies is not:

- A dating app
- A social media feed
- An influencer platform
- A marketplace
- A popularity contest
- A location-tracking tool

---

## Target Users

Samesies serves:

- Hobbyists looking for local companions
- People with niche interests
- Beginners seeking guidance
- Experienced enthusiasts who enjoy sharing knowledge
- People new to an area looking for activity-based friendships

---

## MVP Goals

The first version should allow users to:

1. Create an account
2. Verify a phone number
3. Select hobbies and interests
4. Set an approximate location
5. Discover nearby people with shared interests
6. Send mutual connection requests
7. Participate in public hobby gatherings

---

## Trust Model

Trust is built through participation.

Questions the platform attempts to answer:

- Is this account controlled by a real person?
- Did the interaction actually occur?
- Is community feedback independent and credible?
- Has the member behaved consistently over time?

Trust levels:

- New
- Developing
- Established
- Long-Standing

These levels describe platform history, not personal character or safety.

---

## Safety Philosophy

Safety should be built into the product without making the experience feel hostile.

Key principles:

- Approximate locations only
- Public-location first meetups
- Mutual connections before messaging
- Block and report tools everywhere
- Human review for serious moderation actions

---

## Success Metric

The primary success metric is:

> Two nearby people discover a shared interest, meet safely, and choose to do that hobby together again.

---

## Current Status

Phase: Concept Development

Current priorities:

- Branding
- Domain and trademark research
- Technical architecture
- MVP planning
- Early validation

---

## Future Question

Every feature should answer:

> Does this help someone discover a genuine local Samesie, connect safely, and enjoy a shared interest in real life?

If not, it probably does not belong in the MVP.
