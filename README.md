# Samesies

The local side of shared interests.

Samesies is a privacy-first platform that helps people discover nearby people who share their hobbies, interests, passions, and wonderfully specific obsessions.

## Mission

Help people find local hobby friends.

## Why?

It's easy to find thousands of people online who share an interest.

It's surprisingly difficult to find the handful of people nearby who share that same interest and might actually want to do it together.

## Core Principles

- Local first
- Privacy first
- Behavior over identity
- Real life over screen time
- Evidence over ratings

## North Star

Two nearby people discover a shared interest, meet safely in a public setting, and voluntarily choose to do that hobby together again.

## Status

Pre-development

Currently validating concepts, branding, safety systems, and MVP requirements.
